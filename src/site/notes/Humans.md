Humans come from a land far far away, and all known to Earthbread are either Witches or Wizards. Humans are a traumatized people, and their philosophy reflects this. Witches, other than *The Witch of Light* and her followers, created Cookiekind to create an ideal world. They wanted subjects, creatures lesser than them to serve them, to forever protect them. The Wizards, on the other hand, believed in a completely non-contact relationship with Cookiekind. They saw creating life as sacred, and believed in it thoroughly.

*The Witch of Light* believed in freedom for Cookiekind, and her and her followers did everything they could to protect it.
