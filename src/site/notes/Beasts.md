With the absence of the Witches and the recession of Wizards from Cookie society, the Witches knew that Cookiekind needed gods to rule over the land. While there was Sugar Swan Cookie and *The Guardians of Nature*, their tasks were inherently ethereal and had nothing to do with the society and safety of Cookiekind. So, The Five Virtues were created. These beings are not natural, they were not born. They were created, created with more power then any Cookie could possibly have, they were gods. Their power originated in their Soul Jam, a special extension of their Soulstones.

The world run under The Five Virtues was a paradise. But it did not last long. One by one, beginning with Blueberry Milk Cookie, each Virtue slowly began to lose themselves. With no mortality to attach to, they could never understand humanity truly, and they each became a mockery of what they once were.

They were sealed away by one of their own, Silent Salt Cookie, who along with Elder Faerie Cookie used the forbidden magic of the witches to end the reign of the Beasts for good. For hundreds of years, Cookiekind was at a relative peace, the Beasts sealed under the Silver Tree. The lands, now wild, that they once ruled became known as Beast-Yeast, the corpse of the Beasts.

## HERALD OF CHANGE - GOLDEN SPICE COOKIE
![burningspice2.png\|300](/img/user/burningspice2.png)
Golden Spice Cookie was a kind and caring god. He oversaw the rise and fall of civilizations, and he was an ally to all of them. He would, usually, remain impartial to societies. He had a sprawling temple where he, along with the *Angels of the Spicelands*, would celebrate humanity and history.

But, over time, the heartache of watching those he loved outlive him, their bloodlines outlive him, and then the mere memory of them outlive him... he began to grow jaded.
## BEAST OF DESTRUCTION - BURNING SPICE COOKIE
![burningspice1.png\|300](/img/user/burningspice1.png)
Burning Spice Cookie is not a kind nor merciless god. There is one thing that entertains him. Destruction.

He has grown tired of seeing the cycle of change, so now he will use it to destroy it. He resides in his temple with his generals, finding societies further and further from the center of the Spicelands to destroy.
